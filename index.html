About Me


Hi! I'm Thảo, a recent AI Master’s graduate from Singapore Management University, currently exploring roles across Machine Learning Engineering, AI/Solutions Engineering, and product-facing AI. I enjoy working at the intersection of business problems and applied machine learning: translating ambiguous, messy data into clear insights and reliable ML systems that can actually ship.
